## How to Select the Right 32 ECT Box

A box's ECT rating is only one part of the selection process. Consider the full packaging requirement before choosing a carton.

**Measure the contents.** Record the product's length, width, and height, including any protruding parts. Allow enough room for cushioning if the item is fragile or easily scratched.

**Check the packed weight.** The weight of the product alone does not determine whether a 32 ECT box is suitable. Consider how the load is distributed and whether the box will be stacked under other cartons.

**Understand the shipping environment.** Parcel shipments, palletized loads, long-term storage, and humid conditions can place different demands on corrugated packaging. Extended storage and moisture exposure may reduce box strength.

**Choose suitable internal protection.** Bubble packaging, foam, dividers, or paper cushioning may help prevent products from shifting or contacting the carton walls. The appropriate material depends on the product.

**Consider stacking and palletizing.** If cartons will be stored in several layers, evaluate the combined load and stacking arrangement. A higher ECT rating or different board construction may be necessary for demanding conditions.

**Seal and handle boxes correctly.** Use a suitable closure method, distribute contents evenly, and avoid overfilling. Damaged corners, poorly sealed flaps, and unsupported gaps can affect package performance.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### 1. What does 32 ECT mean?

ECT stands for Edge Crush Test. A 32 ECT rating describes the edge-compression resistance of corrugated board, providing information relevant to box stacking performance.

### 2. Does a 32 ECT box hold only 32 pounds?

No. The number is not a direct product-weight limit. The suitable load depends on factors such as box construction, dimensions, contents, stacking conditions, and handling.

### 3. Are 32 ECT boxes suitable for e-commerce shipping?

They can be suitable for many routine e-commerce shipments when the box dimensions and construction match the product's weight and protection needs. Fragile items may need internal cushioning.

### 4. Can 32 ECT boxes be stacked on pallets?

They can be used in palletized shipments when the box and load configuration are appropriate. Evaluate the number of layers, total weight, storage duration, and environmental conditions before choosing a board rating.

### 5. What products can be packed in 32 ECT boxes?

Common applications include retail goods, accessories, printed materials, household items, and selected industrial components. Product weight, fragility, and shipping conditions determine suitability.

### 6. Are 32 ECT boxes recyclable?

Corrugated boxes are commonly recyclable where local collection facilities accept them. Remove or separate materials that local recycling guidelines do not accept, and avoid recycling boxes heavily contaminated with unsuitable substances.

### 7. Do fragile products need extra protection inside a 32 ECT box?

Often, yes. Use suitable cushioning, dividers, or inserts to limit movement and reduce the chance of damage during handling. The carton alone may not provide enough impact protection.

### 8. How do 32 ECT and 200# test ratings differ?

They measure different properties. ECT measures edge-compression resistance, while the Mullen burst test measures resistance to bursting under pressure. Certain single-wall grades may be used for similar applications, but the ratings are not numerically interchangeable.
